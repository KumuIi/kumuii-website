ddlc kinda broke my brain (in a good way)
no date · #origin #weird

ok so the whole "kumuii likes unsettling stuff" thing... it started w doki doki literature club. i went in for the cute characters w literally NO idea what i signed up for, n somehow loving it MORE because i didnt see it coming is what flipped a switch in me. like oh. this is what i want. give me the thing that looks one way n then is something else entirely.

thats kinda the root of all of it. the dystopian stuff, the liminal places, the games w that weird lonely feel. i just chase that unsettled but in a way i love feeling everywhere now. ill admit it im also drawn to the intense devoted girl characters who feel TOO much, the unpredictable ones... idk what that says about me lol but its honest so its going here :p

peripeteia is basically my whole personality
no date · #games #goat

my all time fav game n its not close. i played it at a time where nothing hit anymore, everything just holds your hand n points a big beacon at where u gotta go. peripeteia doesnt do that. it just DROPS you into this endless grimy soviet city w no real objective n lets you explore n fight n discover n learn about the world on your own. making mistakes is fine. getting lost is the point.

n the lowpoly jank?? thats not a flaw thats the soul. it feeds the whole thing. i was excited to boot it up like i was a kid in gradeschool rushing home to play fortnite again. thats the feeling i thought i lost. PLAY IT. its also basically the blueprint for the game im making lol.

why my whole game runs on a flip phone
no date · #untitled_angel #devlog

so UNTITLED_ANGEL (placeholder name, dont judge) is an immersive sim where you play as just some dude n it becomes about who YOU turn into. but the heart of it is the flip phone. you build basically every relationship through texting, multiple choice, think needy streamer overload meets persona. cause lets be real... we text people way more than we actually see them, so why not make THAT the whole thing.

n it matters. what you say, how fast you reply, whether you leave someone on read. but you still meet up n make plans irl, n thats where you really learn their fears n obsessions n traumas, n maybe fall for them. flip phone idea + name came from a game called mamas sleeping angel. also flip phones are just cool ok :P its my dream project, slowly cooking.

the notebook novel (no spoilers i promise)
no date · #writing #psychological

im writing a psychological novel. premise: a boys notebook gets stolen, the one where he wrote what he ACTUALLY thinks about everyone he knows, n suddenly its all out there. hes the kind of guy who becomes whoever hes around, n the story is about that catching up w him.

funny thing is it started as a throwaway draft where the kid just dies, like a quick thing built to be thrown out, but people read it n were like nah keep him alive we wanna know more. so here we are. core idea im chewing on: youre not the main character of everyone elses story. thats all youre getting, no spoilers :p

moths are perfect actually
no date · #unprompted #moth

nobody asked but i LOVE moths. like genuinely obsessed. its not even one thing, its how many cool features these lil guys are just BORN with. the big feathery antennae that can pick up a scent from way far off, the fact that some of them dont even have mouths n just exist to vibe n live their short life, the camouflage where they straight up cosplay as bark or leaves or even other animals, the way they fly completely silent, n yeah ok the wing dust thing is cool too but thats like one item on a long list.

n on top of ALL that theyre also just cute?? fuzzy little night guys w big eyes. unreal creature. if we ever talk n you wanna instantly be my friend just send me a cool moth, thats the cheat code :p
